# Challenge menu

Use these as spoken prompts when groups choose their problem. Do not show every detail at once; the deck’s five-item list is sufficient.

## 1. Monday-Morning Decision Brief

A leadership team receives project updates, customer concerns, staffing notes, and financial signals—but no one knows what needs a decision first.

Build a tool that turns those inputs into a short, evidence-grounded decision brief with owners and next steps.

## 2. Project Rescue Room

A high-visibility project is late. Scope has grown, a vendor is behind, budget is tight, and a customer deadline is fixed.

Build a tool that makes recovery options and their tradeoffs visible before leadership commits.

## 3. AI Adoption Council

A business unit wants to use AI for a consequential process. It needs a practical answer—not just enthusiasm or fear.

Build a tool that captures the use case, expected value, data boundary, failure modes, human review, and rollout decision.

## 4. Management Accelerator

A manager has goals, meetings, commitments, blockers, and a team—but the operating cadence is scattered and reactive.

Build a tool that helps convert those signals into priorities, commitments, and a clear weekly management rhythm.

## 5. AI Make-vs-Buy Studio

An organization is considering an AI capability. It could build internally, buy software, partner, or wait.

Build a tool that makes the decision criteria explicit: value, speed, data fit, integration, operating cost, risk, and reversibility.
